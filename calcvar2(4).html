Calculus of Variations, PDEs and Geometric Analysis

My research develops analytical and geometric methods for nonlinear partial differential equations arising in continuum mechanics, defect theory, image processing and shape optimization.

Functional Spaces, Rigidity and Variational Analysis

A major part of this work concerns BV, BD, BDdev and spaces of bounded 𝒜-variation. These spaces describe discontinuities, singular measures and irreversible deformations while retaining the differential structure of the underlying models.

Blow-Up Methods and Integral Representation

Blow-up arguments identify the local structure of singular measures and energy densities. Integral-representation results then characterize local lower-semicontinuous functionals by bulk, jump and Cantor contributions.

Relaxation, Lower Semicontinuity and Homogenization

Relaxation determines the effective energy of non-convex models under weak convergence. Γ-convergence and homogenization describe effective continuum behaviour generated by fine-scale oscillations, concentrations and heterogeneous microstructures.

Jacobians, Currents and Topological Singularities

Weak Jacobians and currents provide stable representations of topological charge in BV fields. This connects geometric measure theory with vortices, dislocations and other singular structures.

Gradient-Free Models and Image Processing

With S. Amstutz and A. A. Novotny, I developed gradient-free approximations of perimeter-type energies for topology optimization, image segmentation and classification.
