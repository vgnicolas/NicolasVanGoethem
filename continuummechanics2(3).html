Research Area

Continuum Mechanics of Defective Solids

My research develops mathematically rigorous models for defective continua, combining continuum mechanics, variational methods, partial differential equations and numerical approximation.

Incompatibility, Dislocations and Plasticity

A central direction, developed with S. Amstutz and more recently with T.-N. Lê, concerns incompatibility-governed models for elasticity and elasto-plasticity. Incompatibility links dislocation densities to elastic and plastic strain fields and permits defects to enter directly into the continuum description.

A small-strain formulation based on incompatibility as the primary kinematic descriptor of plastic deformation.

This programme builds on Kröner’s theory and on earlier work in crystal growth, where the absence of a natural undeformed and stress-free reference configuration motivated intrinsic formulations in terms of strain and its derivatives.

Incompatibility and Elasticity

My work investigates intrinsic formulations in which elastic strain, rather than displacement, is the primary kinematic variable. This includes analytical results for the incompatibility operator, the Frank tensor, Beltrami-type decompositions and compatible–incompatible decompositions of symmetric tensor fields.

At a geometric level, incompatibility is the linearization of Riemann curvature, whereas dislocations are associated with torsion. Their interaction naturally belongs to non-Riemannian differential geometry.

Analysis and Numerical Approximation

Analysis, finite-element approximation, and numerical simulation of second-gradient incompatible elasticity and elasto-plasticity, with discretizations tailored to the incompatibility operator and benchmarking against classical formulations.

Current work uses structure-preserving discretizations derived from differential complexes. The numerical programme includes benchmark problems involving dislocations, size effects, strain localization and gradient elasto-plasticity.

Functional Spaces in Elasticity and Elastoplasticity

Recent work on BDdev(Ω), with M. Caroccia, concerns rigidity and functional properties of fields of bounded deviatoric deformation. Related work on integral representation in BD provides a basis for relaxation and homogenization in variational models of elasticity and plasticity.
