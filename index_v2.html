About

I develop mathematical models for solids with defects, combining rigorous analysis with continuum mechanics and numerical approximation.

I am an Associate Professor at the Department of Mathematical Sciences of the Faculty of Sciences, University of Lisbon, and a researcher at CEMSUL.

My work lies at the interface of applied analysis, materials science and computation. It addresses elasticity, elasto-plasticity, fracture, damage and dislocation theory through variational methods, partial differential equations, geometric measure theory, differential geometry and topological sensitivity analysis.

03

Current research

Primary programme

Incompatibility-governed elasto-plasticity

Developing a small-strain theory in which incompatibility is placed at the heart of plastic deformation, rather than treated only as a derived consequence of a plastic strain field.

Analysis & numerics

Second-gradient models

Studying higher-order incompatible elasticity and elasto-plasticity, together with finite-element discretizations adapted to the incompatibility operator.

Variational structures

BD, rigidity and homogenization

Investigating rigidity, blow-up arguments, integral representation and homogenization in spaces of bounded deformation and under differential constraints.
