About

I am Professor Auxiliar at the Department of Mathematics of the Faculty of Sciences, University of Lisbon, and a researcher at CMAFcIO.

My research lies at the interface between applied mathematics, continuum mechanics, and materials science. I work on the mathematical modelling of solids with defects, including fracture, damage, dislocations, elasticity and plasticity, using variational methods, partial differential equations, geometric measure theory and differential geometry.

Research Interests

Continuum Mechanics

Mathematical modelling of solids, elasticity, plasticity and thermodynamics.

Fracture & Damage

Variational approaches to fracture mechanics and damage evolution.

Dislocation Theory

Geometric and analytical models of crystalline defects.

Optimization

Shape optimization, topology optimization and Γ-convergence.

PDEs

Elliptic and parabolic equations, functional analysis and applications.

Differential Geometry

Geometry of defective crystals and non-Riemannian structures.
